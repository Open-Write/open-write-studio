## The Voice: V1-A — The Immediate Reckoning

**POV:** First-person retrospective. Martín, older (~30 years after the war), looking back.
**Tense:** Past for the remembered events. Present for the act of writing.
**Register:** Confessional, intimate, the weight of hindsight — but with an immediacy technique that collapses the distance between remembering and remembered self.

### Core Technique: The Immediacy Collapse

The narrator does not simply remember — he re-enters. The body's sensory memory bridges the gap between past and present. When the smell of the trench rises, he is in the trench. When the cold of the parapet touches his hand, the hand is on the parapet and on the table simultaneously.

**Key phrases from the tournament:**
- "I can feel those ridges now. Here. At this table."
- "The pen moves and the boy moves with it. The distance collapses."
- "The body does not file its memories in order. The body dumps them all on the floor and says: sort it out."
- "The hand on the parapet. The hand on the table. The same hand, separated by thirty years and a war."

### The Grounding Location

The narrator writes at his father's table in the hardware store in Pamplona. The inkwell, the funnel, the rag, the crack in the wood — these are the physical anchors of the act of writing. The ritual of filling the inkwell is the ritual of looking.

**Key phrases:**
- "I am writing this in the back room of the hardware store."
- "The inkwell is full. I filled it this morning before I sat down, the way my father taught me."
- "The chair is uncomfortable, the way my father said a chair should be — a chair that is too comfortable is a chair that does not respect the work."

### The Numbness Insight

The voice's most distinctive moral contribution. The body learns to stop feeling — and that learning is the real wound. Not the bullet, not the fear, but the acceptance.

**Key phrases:**
- "Not that the fear stays, but that it goes. Because when the fear goes, the numbness comes, and the numbness is the thing that lets a man do what the war will ask him to do."
- "The numbness came not as relief but as surrender — the body's admission that this was the world now."
- "The acceptance was the wound. Not the bullet. The acceptance."

### The Writing-as-Reckoning Frame

The act of writing is itself the moral labor. The fighting required only the body; the writing requires the willingness to look.

**Key phrases:**
- "The writing will be harder than the fighting, because the fighting required only the body and the writing requires the thing the body cannot provide: the willingness to look at what happened and call it what it was."
- "I am looking. I am calling it."
- "The king did not come."

### Rhythm

- Longer sentences that carry the freight of reflection, broken by short declaratives when memory sharpens into image.
- The syntax of someone sorting through what they remember and what they've invented.
- Fragments when the emotion is too much for complete sentences: "The beret. The crucifix. The king who did not come."

### Vocabulary

Plain, direct, occasionally elevated when the subject demands it — but the elevation is earned, not decorative. The language of a man who has read his Bible and his breviary and has no patience for ornament. Concrete nouns dominate: the road, the beret, the rifle, the crucifix, the table, the inkwell.

### What This Voice Can Do

- Render the irony of a man who now knows what the crusade became, looking at the boy who believed in it.
- Collapse temporal distance through sensory memory — the body bridges thirty years.
- Deliver the numbness insight — the moral center of the novel.
- Make the act of writing itself a dramatic event — the reckoning.

### What This Voice Risks

- Self-pity. Guard against it. The narrator does not pity himself; he looks.
- Indulgence. The immediacy technique must serve the scene, not display itself.
- Editorializing. The narrator does not tell the reader what to feel. He renders what happened.
- Monotony of technique. The "I can feel it now" collapse must vary — sometimes it's smell, sometimes touch, sometimes sound. Never the same bridge twice in a chapter.

### Compatibility with Outline

The outline specifies third-person limited. This voice is first-person retrospective. The voice experiment was conducted after the outline was written; the voice takes precedence. The outline's POV specification is superseded by this locked spec. All prose generation uses first-person retrospective with the immediacy technique.
