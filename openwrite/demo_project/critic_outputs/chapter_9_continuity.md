## 7. STRUCTURAL NOTES

### "The Boy Did Not Know" Refrain (Meta-Critic P2)

Lines 161-163 contain three instances of "The boy did not know":
1. "The boy did not know, then, what his father knew."
2. "The boy did not know any of this."
3. "The boy did not know."

The meta-critic notes flag this as an escalating pattern (15+ cumulative across Ch1-6, max 1 per chapter). Chapter 9 adds 3 more in two lines. **This is structural overuse.** The frame narrator's retrospective knowledge is a legitimate device, but the specific "did not know" construction has been used as a crutch across the manuscript.

**Advisory: Reduce to 1 instance. The other two can be rendered through different construction — e.g., silence, or the letter's weight, or the body's response.**

### Frame-Narrator Ending (Meta-Critic P1)

Lines 171-183: "I am at the table." The frame break enters through a new sensory detail ("Pamplona rain, softer than the Cantabrian mountains gave"). **This satisfies the meta-critic's requirement for new sensory entry.**

The ending's final line: "the silence is the answer and the answer is not enough." This is retrospective narrator interpretive commentary. The meta-critic flags any passage in the final 200 words that "names emotions, explains scenes, or delivers political thesis." "The answer is not enough" is a thesis statement. **Advisory — consider ending on the rendered image (the boy, the stump, the sky) without the interpretive gloss.**

### "Not X" Density

The chapter uses "not" and "did not" extensively:
- "I did not take it out" (line 15)
- "I did not look at them" (line 29)
- "I did not look at the road" → "I looked at the road" (line 29)
- "He did not talk about the cause" (line 27)
- "He did not change them" (line 11)
- "I did not write back" (lines 95, 97, 99)
- "I did not go in" (line 115)
- "I did not count them" (line 125)
- "He did not open it" (line 147)
- "He does not speak" (line 183)
- "The stump does not speak" (line 183)
- "The roots do not speak" (line 183)

The negative construction density is high but serves the chapter's theme of silence, absence, and inability. The repeated "I did not write back" is a deliberate refrain within the chapter. **Advisory — monitor but not blocking, as the negatives serve thematic purpose here.**
