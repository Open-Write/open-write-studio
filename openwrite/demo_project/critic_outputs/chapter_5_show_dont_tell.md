## Verdict: REVISE

19 findings. 5 critical, 8 moderate, 6 advisory. ## Findings

### Critical

**1. Named emotional state: "content"**
- **Location:** Line 21
- **Text:** `His face was content.`
- **Type:** Named emotion (Tier 1 ban — format rule §V)
- **Fix:** `His jaw worked slow. He did not look up from the plate.` — render the satisfaction through behavior, not label it.

**2. Named emotional state: "skeptical" / "open"**
- **Location:** Line 107
- **Text:** `Some faces were still. Some were skeptical. Some were open.`
- **Type:** Named emotional states (Tier 1 ban — format rule §V)
- **Fix:** Replace with physical specifics: `Some jaws were tight. Some leaned forward.` — let the body tell what the faces mean.

**3. Named emotional state: "indifference"**
- **Location:** Line 139
- **Text:** `with indifference, with the knowledge that coins come and go`
- **Type:** Named emotional state + telling-not-rendering (format rule §V)
- **Fix:** Cut the clause entirely. The preceding action already shows it: `He was holding the pin between his thumb and forefinger, the way a man holds a coin.` Full stop.

**4. Telling-not-rendering: naming the bitterness**
- **Location:** Line 183
- **Text:** `The bitterness was in the words. Not between them. In them.`
- **Type:** Telling-not-rendering. The preceding description of the letter — the crowding, the pressure, the word crossed out twice — already renders the shift. Naming it ("bitterness") collapses the rendering into summary.
- **Fix:** Delete the sentence. The physical description of the letter is sufficient. If emphasis needed: `The lines were tight. The ink was heavy.`

**5. Named emotional state: "desperation"**
- **Location:** Line 169
- **Text:** `the words pressed against each other as if the paper were too small for what he needed to say — and Martín knows the man's desperation`
- **Type:** Named emotion — "desperation" (Tier 1 ban — format rule §V). The physical rendering (crowding, tight lines, the bone-folder crease) already shows the urgency. Naming it flattens the effect.
- **Fix:** Delete "and Martín knows the man's desperation." The physical details are the desperation.

### Moderate

**6. Telling-not-rendering: explaining Tomás's pragmatism**
- **Location:** Lines 52–53
- **Text:** `He was practical. The body attended to the task. The task was eggs. The decree was a paper. Papers came and went. The eggs were real.`
- **Type:** Authorial explanation of what Tomás's behavior already demonstrates. The dialogue and action (finding eggs, frying eggs, splitting eggs) already show pragmatism. The narrator then abstracts it into a thesis.
- **Fix:** Cut lines 52–53. Let the scene stand. Tomás talking about the hen while the decree sits on the table is the rendering.

**7. Telling-not-rendering: naming "the pragmatism"**
- **Location:** Line 107
- **Text:** `He was listening the way he listened to Vidal — without ideology, without theology, the pragmatism of a man measuring a thing by its weight.`
- **Type:** Named abstraction ("ideology," "theology," "pragmatism") — the narrator interprets Tomás's listening rather than rendering it. Also a "without X, without Y" construction (Tier 1 ban variant).
- **Fix:** `He was listening the way he listened to Vidal — his eyes on the man's hands, not his mouth.` — ground the assessment in physical behavior.

**8. Telling-not-rendering: explaining the contrast**
- **Location:** Line 155
- **Text:** `The body was arranged around its objects and the objects were arranged around the body and one of the objects was new and the rest were old.`
- **Type:** The narrator names the contrast ("new" / "old") that the physical rendering already established (tin vs. wood, cool vs. warm, manufactured vs. carved). The line explains what the objects already showed.
- **Fix:** End at `The rifle was in the hands.` The preceding paragraph (lines 149–154) already renders the contrast through texture, temperature, and weight.

**9. Authorial intrusion: over-articulating the symbol contrast**
- **Location:** Line 219
- **Text:** `The difference was the difference between a thing that is produced and a thing that is passed down, between a symbol that is distributed from a box and a symbol that is inherited from a hand.`
- **Type:** Authorial intrusion. The narrator steps back to explain the symbolic meaning of the physical contrast — manufactured tin vs. carved wood, box vs. hand — that the objects already embody. This is the narrator telling the reader what to think about what they've already seen.
- **Fix:** Delete entirely. The preceding sentences (corpus carved, lines uneven; pin stamped, precision clean) do the work. Or reduce to: `The pin was stamped. The corpus was carved.`

**10. Telling-not-rendering: naming "the pragmatism" (Tomás sleeping)**
- **Location:** Line 225
- **Text:** `the pragmatism of a man who has decided that tomorrow is tomorrow and tonight is tonight`
- **Type:** Named abstraction. The physical description (face toward wall, hands under chin, scapular on chest) already renders the man at rest. The narrator then interprets it.
- **Fix:** `He slept as he always slept.` Full stop. The body details are sufficient.

**11. Telling-not-rendering: "Facts did not require discussion"**
- **Location:** Line 147
- **Text:** `These were facts. Facts did not require discussion.`
- **Type:** The narrator explains the meaning of Tomás's behavior (eating, adjusting the pin) rather than letting the behavior speak. Tomás's shrug, his "Done," his return to the bread — all of this is the rendering. The closing sentence is editorial.
- **Fix:** Delete `Facts did not require discussion.` End on `The body attended to the task.`

**12. Telling-not-rendering: "The register that was not a voice but an absence"**
- **Location:** Line 213
- **Text:** `The register that was not voice but an absence. The Patriarch had nothing to say because the thing that needed to be said had no words.`
- **Type:** The narrator names the silence and explains its cause. The physical rendering (window, straight back, hands still, face toward glass) is already doing the work. The second sentence is thesis.
- **Fix:** Delete both sentences. Let `The straight back, the hands still, the face turned toward the glass.` end the paragraph.

### Advisory

**13. Polysyndeton chain: default rhythm**
- **Location:** Line 55
- **Text:** `The beret was on my head and the crucifix was in my pocket and the word was in my chest.`
- **Type:** Polysyndeton chain — the "and...and...and" pattern recurs throughout the chapter (lines 55, 119, 155, 207, 227, 229). When used as default rhythm rather than deliberate emphasis, it becomes a tic. Format rule §IV Tier 2.
- **Fix:** The pattern works at key moments (the closing sequences). At line 55, which is mid-chapter transitional, consider breaking it: `The beret was on my head. The crucifix was in my pocket. The word was in my chest.`

**14. Verbatim refrain: "The body attended to the task"**
- **Location:** Lines 21, 53, 147
- **Type:** Intra-chapter refrain — same phrase 3 times. Format rule §XI flags 3+ instances. This is borderline structural (it tracks Tomás's pragmatism) but risks becoming a stamp. Monitor in full-manuscript lint.
- **Fix:** Reduce to 2 instances. Cut the line 53 instance (which is already in a telling-not-rendering passage — see finding #6).

**15. Verbatim refrain: "The pin was on the beret. The beret was on the head."**
- **Location:** Lines 151, 155, 207, 221, 227, 231
- **Type:** Intra-chapter refrain — 6 instances. This is clearly structural (the objects arranging around the body) but at 6 repetitions in 241 lines it risks becoming mechanical. Format rule §XI.
- **Fix:** Reduce to 3–4 instances at the most structurally significant moments (first pinning, the crucifix comparison, the closing). Cut or vary at lines 155, 207, 227.

**16. Omniscience leak: narrator observes Martín from outside**
- **Location:** Lines 238–239
- **Text:** `The boy moves with it. He is twenty-two and the fire is dying...`
- **Type:** POV contract violation (format rule §IX). The narrator shifts from Martín's first-person consciousness to an external view of "the boy." This is omniscient narration observing the character from outside — the narrator sees Martín's age, his position, his objects, as if watching from across the room. The chapter is Martín's close first person; this line steps outside.
- **Fix:** `I was twenty-two. The fire was dying. The artillery was in the walls. The pin was on the beret.` — keep inside the POV contract.

**17. Authorial meta-commentary: "The same hand"**
- **Location:** Line 238
- **Text:** `The hand that held the pin. The hand that held the crucifix. The same hand.`
- **Type:** The narrator articulates the chapter's thesis. The entire chapter has rendered this contrast through objects, textures, temperature, weight. The closing line names it. This is meta-address — the narrator stepping back to state the theme (format rule §IV, Tier 1).
- **Fix:** The objects have already done this work. End on the physical: `The pen moves.` or `I did not know.` (line 241 is a strong close — the intervening thesis weakens it).

**18. Hedge / qualifying construction: "as if"**
- **Location:** Line 169
- **Text:** `the words pressed against each other as if the paper were too small for what he needed to say`
- **Type:** "As if" construction — format rule §IV Tier 3, monitor if >1 per page. This is the only instance in the letter section, so it passes, but the simile is doing work the physical description already does (crowding, tight lines).
- **Fix:** Minor. Could be tightened: `the words pressed against each other, the paper too small for what he needed to say.`

**19. Telling-not-rendering: "the pull. The gravity of an argument"**
- **Location:** Line 111
- **Text:** `I felt it. The pull. The gravity of an argument that was articulate and complete and not mine.`
- **Type:** The narrator names the sensation ("pull," "gravity") and then interprets it ("an argument that was articulate and complete and not mine"). The preceding rendering of Salazar's voice — its precision, its rhythm, its filling the room — already shows the persuasion. Naming it reduces the effect.
- **Fix:** End at `I felt it.` or render through body: `My hand found the beret. The pin was there.`

**Pattern:** The chapter's physical rendering is excellent — the eggs, the pin, the crucifix, the letter are all rendered with specificity and distance modulation. The failures cluster in two areas:

1. **Naming after showing.** The narrator renders a physical contrast (tin vs. wood, crowded ink vs. straight strokes, eggs vs. decree) and then names the emotional or symbolic meaning. This is the most frequent pattern — findings #1, #4, #5, #6, #7, #8, #9, #10, #11, #12, #19 all share this structure.

2. **Refrain accumulation.** The "objects arranged around the body" pattern and "The body attended to the task" carry structural weight but at 6 and 3 instances respectively risk mechanical repetition.

**High-risk zone (lines 205–241):** 3 findings (#16 omniscience leak, #17 meta-commentary thesis, #14 refrain at line 227). The final line ("I did not know") is clean. The weakness is in the lines immediately preceding it, where the narrator articulates the chapter's thesis rather than letting the objects carry it to the close.
