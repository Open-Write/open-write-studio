## Verdict: FAIL — 19 findings (6 critical, 8 moderate, 5 advisory)

The chapter's first two-thirds are largely clean — concrete, physical, rendered through Martín's senses. The problems concentrate in two zones: the **mid-chapter interpretive passages** (lines 73–95, 131–149) where the narrator steps back to explain, and the **final 15%** (lines 178–209), which collapses into authorial summary and banned constructions. The ending undoes much of the discipline that precedes it.

## CRITICAL FINDINGS (6)

### C-1 · Banned Construction: "Not X but Y" (×6 instances)

The chapter uses "Not X but Y" / "Not X. Not Y. Z." six times. This is a Tier 1 absolute-ban construction per format rules §IV.

**Instance 1 — Line 131–133:**
> "The silence was not the silence of waiting. It was the silence of absence."

**Instance 2 — Line 133:**
> "the warmth that was not warmth but the absence of cold"

**Instance 3 — Line 133:**
> "Not dead. Frozen."

**Instance 4 — Line 169:**
> "the war was not something to be won but something to be ended"

**Instance 5 — Line 199:**
> "The homecoming was not a return. It was a recognition."

**Instance 6 — Line 201:**
> "not the silence of estrangement but the silence of two men who knew the same thing"

**Fix:** Rewrite each instance for directness. For C-1/5/6 (the abstract interpretive ones), cut entirely — the scene has already rendered these truths. For C-1/2/3 (the physical ones), convert to concrete action: render the warmth through Tomás's shoulder pressure, render the frozen prayer through the body's stillness in the pew.

### C-2 · Authorial Summary — Chapter Ending (lines 199–205)

The final 15% abandons rendering for interpretive summary. The narrator steps outside Martín's moment-to-moment consciousness to explain the chapter's meaning.

**Line 199–201:**
> "The distance between what was and what I was could not be crossed by a train or a walk or a door or a bed. The distance was the war. The distance was the cold. The distance was the boy I shot and the man I held and the frozen dead and the fueros that were being discussed and the king who did not come."

This is the narrator cataloguing abstract nouns. Every item in the list has already been rendered concretely earlier in the chapter. The summary adds nothing the reader doesn't already know.

**Line 201:**
> "The thing we knew was that the thing we had fought for was gone. The thing we knew was that the crucifix was on the wall and the beret was on the hook and the king was not coming and the fueros were not being discussed well and the oak in the plaza had been cut and the tradition was a century old and the century was over."

Authorial summary disguised as repetition. The narrator is explaining what Martín and his father understood — a job the scene already did through the dining table, the crucifix, the window, the silence.

**Line 203:**
> "The restraint that was love in July of 1936 — the hands on my head, the press above the ear, the step back — was distance in March of 1938."

The narrator is interpreting the symbolic meaning of the beret gesture. This is the narrator's job — but it should be rendered through Martín's body at the corner, not through abstractions.

**Fix:** Cut lines 199–205. End the chapter at line 197 ("The wool remembers.") or at line 189 ("The road was ahead."). The beret-on-the-hook image (line 197) is a stronger ending than the interpretive coda. If the coda is kept, compress to one sentence of rendered consciousness — Martín's hand on the beret, the fold under his fingers, the road ahead.

### C-3 · Omniscience Leak — Father's Motivation (lines 75, 95)

The narrator attributes internal states to Don Eusebio that Martín cannot know.

**Line 75:**
> "He did not ask because he did not need to ask. The asking would have been a cruelty. The answers would have changed nothing."

Martín cannot know why his father didn't ask. He can observe the silence, the bread, the wine — but the reason ("the asking would have been a cruelty") is the narrator's interpretation, not Martín's perception.

**Line 95:**
> "the rest was not his to know. Not because he did not care. Because knowing would not help."

Same issue. Martín observes the nod, the absence of follow-up. The explanation ("not because he did not care") is the narrator stepping outside Martín's POV.

**Fix:** Render through Martín's observation. The father's face, his hands, the deliberate return to the bread — these convey the same truth without the narrator explaining it. Cut the interpretive sentences.

### C-4 · Meta-Address — Narrator Explains Structure (lines 7, 17, 145)

The narrator steps back to address the reader about the chapter's own construction.

**Line 7:**
> "I am going to write about going home. Not the going — the arriving. The leaving was simple. The arriving was not."

**Line 17:**
> "I want to say this plainly, because the expectation is that the man returns from the war and the city is transformed — smaller, or larger, or different in some way that justifies the distance that has opened."

**Line 145:**
> "The days passed. I will not catalogue them."

All three are meta-address — the narrator explaining what the chapter will do or won't do. Tier 1 ban per format rules §IV.

**Fix:** Line 7 — cut or convert to rendered moment (Martín at the inkwell, the pen hovering). Line 17 — cut; the concrete detail of Pamplona being unchanged already conveys this. Line 145 — cut; transition directly to the scene.

### C-5 · Authorial Interpretation — "The Looking Was His Labor" (line 149)

> "I watched him from the doorway. The window was his place. The looking was his labor — the labor of a man watching a century of faith being dismantled in front of him, piece by piece, decree by decree, and saying nothing because the saying would change nothing."

The first two sentences are rendered perception. The third is the narrator interpreting Don Eusebio's internal state — "a century of faith being dismantled" is not something Martín can see from a doorway. It's the narrator's thesis.

**Fix:** Cut after "The looking was his labor." The image of the father at the window, the stoop, the hands behind his back, the berets passing — that's enough. The reader understands.

### C-6 · Named Interpretation — "Finish" (line 169)

> "Not fight. Not win. Finish. The word was the word of a man who understood that the war was not something to be won but something to be ended. The winning had already been taken from them. The ending was all that remained."

The father says "Finish." The narrator then explains what the word means — three sentences of interpretation. This is "Not X but Y" construction (Tier 1 ban) layered over authorial intrusion. The word "Finish" is strong enough to stand alone.

**Fix:** Cut lines 169 after "Finish." Let the word land. The scene — the window, the oak, the fueros — has already given the reader everything needed to understand it.

## MODERATE FINDINGS (8)

### M-1 · Telling — "I was the one who had changed" (line 19)

> "I was the one who had changed."

Declarative summary of the chapter's theme. The preceding paragraph (Pamplona unchanged) and the following paragraph (the berets, the yoke-and-arrows) already render this.

**Fix:** Cut. The contrast is already visible.

### M-2 · Telling — "A recognition so sharp it was close to pain" (line 27)

> "The space behind my eyes had been empty for twenty months and it filled and the filling was not comfort. It was a recognition so sharp it was close to pain."

"Recognition" is an abstract noun. "Close to pain" is hedging. The physical detail before it (the smell of iron and oil, the dust motes) is doing the work.

**Fix:** Cut the last sentence. End on "and the filling was not comfort" — or better, render the physical response: what does the body do when the smell hits?

### M-3 · Telling — "Not a question. Not an exclamation. A naming." (line 37)

> "Not a question. Not an exclamation. A naming."

The narrator is labeling the father's utterance. "Martín" is already on the page — the reader can hear the tone.

**Fix:** Cut. The dialogue tag and the period do the work.

### M-4 · Telling — "I knew" (line 73)

> "I did not ask what he meant. I knew."

The following sentences (the fueros being discussed by men who weren't Carlist) render the knowledge. "I knew" is a bridge that tells.

**Fix:** Cut "I knew." Start the next sentence directly.

### M-5 · Telling — "I had grown" (line 107)

> "The room had not shrunk. I had grown. Not in height. In the number of things I had seen. In the distance between the boy who had left this room and the man who stood in its doorway now."

"I had grown" is abstract. The final sentence is good rendering, but the middle is telling.

**Fix:** Cut "I had grown. Not in height. In the number of things I had seen." Keep the last sentence.

### M-6 · Telling — "The body did not know it was home" (line 113)

> "The body was in the bed but the body was also in the trench, on the ridge, in the frozen street. The body did not know it was home."

The first sentence renders concretely. The second names the state.

**Fix:** Cut the second sentence. The trench/ridge/frozen street image is sufficient.

### M-7 · Telling — "The silence of absence" (line 131)

> "The silence was not the silence of waiting. It was the silence of absence. The place where the prayer had been was empty."

"Not X but Y" construction plus abstract naming. "Absence" is not a rendered state.

**Fix:** Cut lines 131–132. Start at "The God was still there." The kneeling, the clasped hands, the empty pew — these render the absence.

### M-8 · Authorial Intrusion — "I think the cold did this" (line 133)

> "I think the cold did this."

The narrator is explaining causation. The cold is already rendered through the body in the pew, the frozen canteens, the trench.

**Fix:** Cut. The metaphor that follows (frozen water in the canteens) does the work without the narrator pointing at it.

## ADVISORY FINDINGS (5)

### A-1 · Triadic Parallel — "the distance of..." (line 187)

> "the distance of the street and the distance of the twenty months and the distance of the war and the distance of the fueros being discussed in Burgos and the distance of the oak that had been cut and the distance of the king who had not come"

Six-fold anaphora. The rhythm is deliberate and the moment is climactic, but the accumulation tips into authorial catalogue. Consider cutting to three.

### A-2 · Repetition of "The crucifix was the same" / "I was not" (lines 81, 87)

> "The crucifix was the same. I was not."

Effective the first time. The second instance (line 87: "I was twenty-three. My father was fifty-nine. The crucifix was old. The king had not come.") works differently — it's compressed and earned. No fix needed, but monitor for pattern fatigue across chapters.

### A-3 · "The voice of the Patriarch" (line 65)

> "measured, declarative, the voice of the Patriarch"

"Measured" and "declarative" are abstract voice descriptors. "The voice of the Patriarch" is a character-specific detail that works. Consider cutting the first two adjectives.

### A-4 · Triadic Closing — "The beret is on the hook. The crucifix is on the wall. The inkwell is full." (line 207)

Three declarative sentences. The triad is structurally intentional (it mirrors the chapter's opening), but it risks the "triplet closing" pattern the lints flag. The final line ("The king did not come.") breaks the pattern — which is good.

### A-5 · Future-Tense Summary (line 205)

> "the war that would take Tomás and the war that would end and the war that would leave me here, at this table, with this inkwell, with this pen, with this crack in the wood that is deeper every year"

The narrator telescopes forward in time. This is a valid narratorial move (the framing device is retrospective), but it pulls the reader out of the March 1938 moment. If the coda is kept, consider staying in the present tense of the frame.
