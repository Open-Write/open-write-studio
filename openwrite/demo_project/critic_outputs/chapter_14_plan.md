# Chapter 14 Plan — "The End"

---

## 1. Chapter Number and Title
Chapter 14 — "The End"

## 2. Characters Present
- **Martín Javier Echeverría** (POV) — The Fracture, Silence emerging
- **Salazar** — brief appearance, his private register
- **Tomás** — referenced, dead

## 3. Outline Summary
March–April 1939. The war ends. Franco enters Madrid on March 28. The requeté are not in Madrid — they are in a garrison in Aragon, waiting for orders. The news comes by radio. The men cheer. Some weep. Martín stands outside and listens. He does not cheer. He does not weep. He holds the crucifix. The war is over. The crusade has succeeded. There is no Carlist king. There is no theocratic monarchy. There is Franco. Salazar finds Martín in the evening. He says: "We built a new Spain." Martín says nothing. Salazar walks away. The chapter's final image: the requeté pack their equipment. The red berets go into the packs. The yoke-and-arrows remain on the caps. The beret is no longer the symbol. The party is the symbol.

## 4. Emotional Palette
**Emptiness** and **Finality.** The reader should feel the victory arrive — and the absence of what it was supposed to deliver.

## 5. Key Beats
1. The war ends. Radio. The men cheer.
2. Martín does not cheer. He holds the crucifix.
3. Salazar: "We built a new Spain." Martín says nothing.
4. The red berets go into the packs. The yoke-and-arrows remain.

## 6. Word Count Target
3,500–4,500 words.

## 7. Body Anchors
Hands (the crucifix, the beret going into the pack), ears (the radio, the cheering), eyes (the berets packed, the yoke-and-arrows remaining).

---

## Notes for the Prose Writer

1. **The victory must feel empty.** Not dramatic. Not tragic. Empty. The war is over. The crusade succeeded. And nothing arrived.
2. **Salazar's line must land as fact.** "We built a new Spain." Martín says nothing. The silence is the chapter.
3. **The berets going into the packs is the chapter's central image.** The red beret — the tradition, the century of wars — folded and packed. The yoke-and-arrows remain on the caps. The party has consumed the symbol.
4. **All meta-critic constraints apply.**
