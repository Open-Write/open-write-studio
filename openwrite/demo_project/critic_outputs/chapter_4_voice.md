## VI. Format-Rule Compliance (Tier 1 Banned Constructions)

**Finding 1 — Line 53:**
> "Not walking the way we walked — tired, dusty, the pack dragging on the shoulders, the feet heavy."

**Verdict:** VIOLATION. "Not X but Y" construction in inverted form. Rewrite: describe how they walked directly, without the negation frame.

**Finding 2 — Lines 55–56:**

**Verdict:** VIOLATION. Exact "Not X. Not Y. Z." construction (here: Not X. Z.). The banned pattern. "Something else" is also a specificity dodge — what *was* it?

**Finding 3 — Lines 48–49:**
> "something that was not quite fear and not quite admiration but lived in the space between"

### VI-F. Triadic Parallel as Default Rhythm

**Finding 10 — Line 155:**
> "The artillery was the heartbeat — low, steady, the pulse of a body larger than any I had known. The machine guns were the breath — quick, sharp, the rhythm of lungs working hard. The rifles were the voice — individual, human, the single shot that carried through the roar the way a name carries through a crowd."

Three parallel constructions, each with a body metaphor (heartbeat, breath, voice), each with two adjectives, each with an expansion. This is a triadic parallel used as the passage's structural backbone. The moment *specifically calls for three beats* (three distinct sound sources), so this is **technically permitted** under the rule's exception clause. However, it is the most prominent rhythm in the passage and risks sounding stamped. **Advisory:** Consider varying the structure of the third element to break the pattern.

---

### VI-G. Named Emotions

**Finding 11 — Line 49:**
> "something that was not quite fear and not quite admiration"

"Fear" and "admiration" are named emotions, though hedged by "not quite." The passage tries to avoid naming by negating — but the effect is to name and then deny. Combined with the "Something between X and Y" violation (Finding 3/7), this passage is the chapter's weakest in terms of format compliance. **Recommend:** Render through physical detail — what did the body do when seeing the Regulares? What did the hands do? Where did the eyes go?

## IX. Verdict
