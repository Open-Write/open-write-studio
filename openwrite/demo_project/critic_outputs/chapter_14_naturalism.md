## Em-dash Analysis (43 — threshold ~15–20)

43 em-dashes in 135 lines = 1 em-dash per 3.1 lines. The em-dash functions in this chapter as:

- **Appositive restatement** (most common): "the officer's jacket — the hybrid uniform of the unified party"
- **Explanatory aside**: "One of them — I will not name him; he was young; he meant it"
- **Rhetorical contrast**: "Not the Falangist register — the sharp, rhythmic cadence of ideology"
- **Sentence interrupter**: "Tomás's unfinished sentence — Tell my mother — the words I would carry"

At this density, the em-dash becomes a structural dependency. The chapter cannot build a paragraph without reaching for it. Cut 60% and force the prose to earn its transitions.

## "The" Sentence Starters (24% — threshold ~15%)

89 of 369 sentences begin with "The." This creates a monotonous, reportorial cadence — "The X. The Y. The Z." — that flattens the prose's rhythm. The effect is most visible in the beret-packing sequence (lines 91–99) and the final paragraph (lines 127–131), where nearly every sentence begins with "The."

The fix is not to avoid "The" but to vary sentence openings: participial phrases, character action, temporal markers, interrogative fragments, prepositional phrases. The chapter needs more varied syntactic on-ramps.

---

## Structural Observations

The chapter's content is strong — the beret-packing scene, the Salazar exchange, the courtyard at night. The naturalism failures are not in the material but in the delivery system. The prose relies on a small set of rhetorical moves (negation, triplets, "the way," em-dash appositives, "The" starters) and does not vary them. The result is a chapter that sounds like it was written by a system with good instincts and a narrow toolkit.

## Priority Fixes

1. **Cut "Not X but Y" to 0.** Each instance needs to be restructured. Use image, not syntax, to convey contrast.
2. **Reduce em-dashes from 43 to ≤15.** Convert appositive em-dashes to sentences or prepositional phrases. Convert aside em-dashes to parentheticals or separate beats.
3. **Reduce "the way" from 12 to ≤5.** Replace with "like," "as," direct metaphor, or no comparison marker at all.
4. **Reduce triplets from 16 to ≤5.** Use single images, pairs, or longer accumulations (5–7) to break the pattern.
5. **Reduce negative density.** The 72 "not" instances should drop to ~30. Render loss through what IS present, not through constant denial.
6. **Reduce "The" starters to ≤15%.** Vary sentence openings with action, participle, temporal, or prepositional on-ramps.
7. **Reduce polysyndeton from 6 to ≤2.** Use varied connective structures.
