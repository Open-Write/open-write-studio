# Voice Critic — Chapter 6: The Field of Jarama

**Critic:** Voice
**Chapter:** 006 — The Field of Jarama (277 lines)
**Characters present:** Martín (narrator/POV), Tomás, Vidal, Padre Joaquín
**Voice spec:** V1-A "The Immediate Reckoning" (locked)
**Registers activated:** Soldier (dominant), Catechism (cracking), Companion (Tomás), Officer (Vidal), Pastor/Confessor (Padre Joaquín)

---

## I. Voice Spec Compliance

### The Immediacy Collapse

The chapter opens and closes in the present-tense writing frame ("I am at the table. The inkwell is full. The pen is in my hand.") and delivers the core technique — the body's sensory memory bridging past and present:

> "I want to write about a field. The body gives me the ears first — the ears full of the artillery, the ears full of the sniper cracks and the mortar thumps and the constant, grinding noise of a war that ran around the clock. Before the names, before the count, before the stalemate — the ears. The ears were the body's first memory of Jarama." (lines 5–6)

This is the voice working as specified. The body dumps its memories unsorted. The narrator enters through sound, not chronology. The bridge is sensory and immediate.

### The Numbness Insight

Partially delivered. The chapter renders the body learning to survive — "Body learned. It crouched. It pressed against the wall. It moved below the parapet without being told." (line 117) — but does not yet name the numbness as wound. This is appropriate for Chapter 6 (Act II); the numbness insight belongs to the later reckoning. The body's adaptation is shown, not yet judged.

### The Writing-as-Reckoning Frame

The closing return to the present tense is brief and effective:

> "I am at the table. The inkwell is full. The pen is in my hand. The hardware store is dark.
> I did not know." (lines 273–275)

The two-word sentence "I did not know" is the reckoning in miniature — the older Martín looking at the boy who believed the propaganda. This is the voice spec's central promise delivered.

### Rhythm

Long sentences carry the freight of reflection, broken by short declaratives when memory sharpens into image. The prose distance modulates well — extreme close-up on sensory detail (the sardine oil, the clay under fingernails), middle distance for trench life, compressed lyric for the passage of days. Five consecutive paragraphs at similar distance: not detected.

### Verdict: PASS — Voice spec is honored. The immediacy collapse, grounding location, and reckoning frame are all present and working.

## II. Character Voice Registers

### Martín

**Dominant register: The Soldier.** Concrete, physical, present-tense in its attention. "Short observations. Sensory. No abstraction." (character profile). This is correct for Act II — the Soldier register is strengthening as the war progresses.

**Catechism register surfaces in the confession scene:**

> "I have killed men," I said. (line 157)
> "I was told it was just," I said. "I was told God was with us. I believed it." (line 169)

The Catechism is present but no longer declarative — it is defensive, questioned. The syntax of conviction ("We fight for the king") has become the syntax of justification ("I was told"). This is the register cracking on schedule.

**Assessment:** Martín's voice is consistent with the profile. The Soldier dominates. The Catechism is cracking. The Son appears in the presence of Padre Joaquín (deferential, kneeling). No register violations.

### Tomás

**Register: The Companion.** Easy, colloquial, half-sentences. Dialect present. Humor as deflection.

> "That's our neighbor," he said. "Friendly fellow. Says hello every twenty minutes." (lines 28–29)
> "Not bad," he said. "Compared to Madrid. At least here we can see the sky." (lines 41, 45)
> "She writes that the hen is laying again," he said. "Three eggs last week. She says it's the spring." (lines 207–208)

**The Brother register surfaces in combat:**

> Beside me, Tomás fired — steady, flat, jaw set, eyes on the field. (line 217)

No dialogue in combat — the fear strips the accent, the joking stops. The body takes over. This is exactly the profile's specification.

**The Son register is absent** — appropriate, as it surfaces only in letters home and private moments away from other men.

**Assessment:** Tomás is the novel's most consistent voice. The Companion register carries the chapter. The shift to The Brother in combat is rendered through physical description, not dialogue — correct. One minor note: Tomás's dialogue and Martín's narrative voice occasionally share the same cadence of short declarative observation. This is acceptable in context (Martín is absorbing Tomás's way of seeing through proximity), but worth monitoring.

### Vidal

**Register: The Officer.** Clipped, precise, professional.

> "Here. You're here." (line 19)
> "Bury him tonight. Do not stand above the parapet. Do not reach above the parapet. If you need something, crawl." (line 127)
> "This is not a siege. This is a field. A field does not care which side you are on." (lines 131–132)

**The Realist register surfaces when reading names:**

> Vidal read the names each evening. He stood at the end of the trench, paper in his hand, face flat, voice clipped. Name, hometown, date. (lines 201–202)

No hedging, no comfort. Facts delivered. The requeté fear this register — and the chapter shows why.

**Assessment:** Vidal's voice is precise and distinct from the requeté. The Castilian flatness is present in the prose's rendering of his face ("flat") and voice ("clipped"). No dialect, no invocations, no ideology. PASS.

### Padre Joaquín

**Register: The Pastor** (with cracks showing).

> "God is with you," Padre Joaquín said. His voice was warm, measured, certain. But the certainty was slower. Words came as if from further away. "The cause is just. The Church has said so." (lines 173–174)

**The Confessor register surfaces in the confession scene:**

> "What is your sin, my son?" (line 153)
> "Do you feel remorse?" (line 165)
> "Pray for clarity," he said. "The killing is not a sin if the cause is just. But you must know it is just. Not believe. Know." (lines 176–177)

The "hijo" (son) address is present ("my son"). Questions rather than declarations. The doubt leaks through the pauses — "the certainty was slower," "words came as if from further away," "fingers folding and unfolding." The physical tells (shaking hands, longer pauses) render the doubt through Martín's perception, not through interiority access. This respects the POV contract.

**Assessment:** Padre Joaquín's voice is the chapter's strongest characterization achievement. The Pastor register holds but is visibly eroding. The physical rendering of doubt (hands, pauses, slowness) is exactly what the profile demands at this stage. PASS.

## V. POV Contract

The chapter stays inside Martín's perception throughout. Other characters' interiority is rendered through what Martín sees and hears:

- **Padre Joaquín's doubt:** Rendered through pauses, hand movements, voice slowness — not through interiority access.
- **Tomás's state:** Rendered through face, posture, dialogue — not through thought.
- **Vidal's competence:** Rendered through action and clipped dialogue — not through motivation.

**One exception:** Line 103 — "Men from England were in the trench across the field... We were in holes on opposite sides of a brown field, and the field was worth nothing, and we were killing each other over it." This is Martín's retrospective judgment, not perception. Acceptable in the first-person retrospective frame — the older narrator is allowed to judge.

**Assessment:** PASS. The POV contract is honored.

## VIII. Verdict

**CONDITIONAL PASS.**

The voice spec is honored. The immediacy collapse, grounding location, and reckoning frame are present and effective. Character voice registers are distinct and consistent with profiles. The POV contract is maintained. Dialogue differentiation is strong across all four characters.

Two blocking issues must be resolved before the chapter advances:

1. **Chinese character artifact at line 23** — technical error, not a voice issue, but it breaks the prose.
2. **Four instances of the banned "Not X. Not Y. Z." construction** — reduce to two. The remaining two instances (lines 77, 101) serve the chapter's central thematic function (the collapse of propaganda categories). The other two (lines 17, 107) can be rewritten without loss.

The chapter's voice achievement is significant: it renders the moment when the crusade's language fails against reality, and it does so through the body's senses rather than through declaration. Padre Joaquín's confession scene is the chapter's strongest voice work — the doubt leaking through the Pastor register via physical tells (hands, pauses, slowness) while the voice remains warm and measured. This is the voice spec working at its best.
