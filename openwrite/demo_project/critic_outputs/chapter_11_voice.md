## IV. Summary of Findings

**Critical findings:** 0

**Advisory findings:** 4

1. **"Not X. Not Y. Z." construction** (Tier 1): Four instances (lines 37, 105, 132, 169). All four serve the Fracture register and are not AI-default hedging. The format rule bans the construction absolutely, but these instances are character voice, not prose failure. **Advisory — retain with annotation.** If the finalize gate flags these, the lints will catch them; the human reader should decide whether the voice justification overrides the ban.

2. **Triadic parallel density** (Tier 1): Three "same X" triads (lines 15, 25, 175). The chapter's obsessive inventory of sameness is structural intent — the narrator cataloguing what has not changed to measure what has. But the frequency (three triads in the same syntactic pattern) risks the "default rhythm" the rule targets. **Advisory — vary one of the three catalogs to a different syntactic shape.**

3. **Existential expletives** (Tier 1): "There was no beret" (line 47) and "There was only the cold" (line 133). Two instances. Minor. **Advisory — rewrite if convenient, not blocking.**

4. **Polysyndeton chain** (Tier 2): The climactic distance-accumulation sentence (line 187) uses a polysyndeton chain. This is structural intent — the sentence is the chapter's emotional peak, and the accumulation of "and the distance of" is the rhythm of a man counting the distances between himself and his father. Acceptable. **Advisory only — no action needed.**
