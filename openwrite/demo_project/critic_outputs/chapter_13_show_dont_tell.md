## Critical Findings (6)

### 1. Voice Register Label — "The Confessor"

**Location:** Line 27
**Type:** Authorial intrusion / Format rule violation
**Text:**
> His voice was quiet. The voice of The Confessor — the register he used in private, close, almost whispered. But it was not The Confessor. The Confessor asks questions.

**Rule violated:** Format Rules §VIII ("Voice register names must never appear in the manuscript itself") and §IV Tier 1 ("Meta-address").
**Fix:** Render the voice quality through physical detail and Martín's perception. E.g., drop the label entirely: *His voice was quiet. Close. Almost whispered. The voice he used when there was only one person in the room. But this was not a question. This was a statement.*

## Overall Assessment

The chapter's two-hander structure (Martín and Padre Joaquín in the field hospital) is strong. The dialogue is excellent — Joaquín's fragments ("I blessed the bullets," "I am not sure these are the same blessing") are among the best lines in the manuscript. The problem is the narrator's compulsion to interpret what the scene already shows. The three voice register labels (#1, #2, #3) are the most damaging violations — they break format rules that exist specifically to prevent this. The philosophical generalizations about "the body" (#7, #9, #14, #16, #22) form a pattern that risks making the narrator sound like an essayist rather than a man sitting in a chair holding a crucifix.

The strongest sections are the pure scene work: the physical description of the room (lines 17–21), the dialogue (lines 63–95), and the walk back (lines 197–203 without the interpretation). These passages trust the reader.
