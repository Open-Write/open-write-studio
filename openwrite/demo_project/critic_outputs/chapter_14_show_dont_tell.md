## Violations — Tier 1 Banned Constructions

### 1. Line 11 — "Not X but Y" + omniscience leak

> "the face of a professional soldier watching a war end **the way wars end — not with a final battle but with a series** of surrenders and retreats and radio announcements"

**Type:** Banned construction ("Not X but Y") + generalization beyond narrator's experience.
**Location:** Line 11, mid-paragraph.
**Problem:** "Not X but Y" is Tier 1 banned. Additionally, Martín at 24 has fought in one war; "the way wars end" claims familiarity with how multiple wars conclude. At 56 he might generalize, but the sentence is embedded in the 24-year-old's present-tense observation of Vidal.
**Fix:** Cut "the way wars end — not with a final battle but with a series of." Rewrite: "the face of a professional soldier watching surrenders and retreats and radio announcements that grew longer as the territory grew smaller."

---

### 2. Line 21 — "Not X but Y" + existential expletive

> "**not for warmth but because there is** nowhere else to stand"

**Type:** Banned construction ("Not X but Y") + existential expletive ("there is").
**Location:** Line 21, end of simile.
**Problem:** Two Tier 1 violations in one clause. The simile about gathering around the radio uses the banned negation-then-assertion pattern and the banned existential expletive.
**Fix:** Rewrite: "not for warmth. They had nowhere else to stand." Or cut the simile entirely — the gathering is already rendered by "We gathered around it."

### 3. Line 61 — "Not X. Not Y. Z."

> "**Not sad. Not angry. Something else.** The face of a man who has delivered a parcel to the wrong address and cannot get it back."

**Type:** Banned construction ("Not X. Not Y. Z.").
**Location:** Line 61, Salazar's face.
**Problem:** The format rules ban "Not X but Y" / "Not X. Not Y. Z." in any form. The negation preamble names excluded emotions (sad, angry) before the simile renders the face. The simile is strong enough to stand alone.
**Fix:** Cut "Not sad. Not angry. Something else." Begin directly: "The face of a man who has delivered a parcel to the wrong address and cannot get it back."

## Violations — Telling-Not-Rendering

### 9. Line 33 — Named cognitive state

> "and **I understood** for the first time that those were the same thing and that the understanding changed nothing"

**Type:** Named cognitive state ("I understood").
**Location:** Line 33, Martín by the wall after the announcement.
**Problem:** The understanding is declared, not rendered. "Those were the same thing" is abstract — waiting and not-wanting collapsed into one, but the reader is told this rather than shown what the collapse feels like in the body.
**Fix:** Render the physical moment: what does Martín's hand do on the crucifix? What happens to his breathing? What does the courtyard look like when waiting and not-wanting become indistinguishable? The sentence "I stood by the wall and held the crucifix" already begins the rendering — extend it.

---

### 10. Line 39 — Named emotion + authorial thesis sentence

> "**Grief** and celebration were the same shape that night."

**Type:** Named emotion ("grief") + authorial intrusion (thesis sentence).
**Location:** Line 39, bonfire scene.
**Problem:** "Grief" is a named emotional state — Tier 1 banned per the rendering spectrum. The sentence is also a thesis statement: the narrator steps back to editorialize about the relationship between grief and celebration. The preceding scene (a man crying while another sings, "did not notice or did not care to notice") already renders this.
**Fix:** Cut the thesis sentence. The scene shows it. If the echo is needed, render a specific physical detail: the man's tears catching firelight while the singer's arm rests on his shoulder.

---

### 11. Line 53 — Voice register label

> "**This was the Private Man.** The voice I had heard once before, late at night..."

**Type:** Telling / voice register label.
**Location:** Line 53, Salazar's dialogue.
**Problem:** The format rules state: "Voice register names must never appear in the manuscript itself." "The Private Man" is a register label — it names a mode of speech rather than rendering the shift through vocal quality. Martín is telling us Salazar's voice changed rather than showing us how.
**Fix:** Render the voice shift through its qualities: "His voice dropped. Not the Falangist cadence — the sharp, rhythmic cadence of ideology. Not the clipped sentences of a man enforcing an order. Quieter. The voice of a man speaking to himself."

---

### 12. Line 79 — Named cognitive state (refrain)

> "**understood** that the understanding changed nothing"

**Type:** Named cognitive state ("understood") — second instance (refrain from line 33).
**Location:** Line 79, post-Salazar, by the fountain.
**Problem:** Repetition of the line 33 pattern. "Understood that the understanding" is double-named cognition. The refrain structure is intentional, but the cognitive state is still told rather than rendered.
**Fix:** If the refrain is structurally necessary, vary the rendering: "The crucifix was smooth. The stars were out. Nothing had changed. Nothing would."

---

### 13. Line 99 — Named cognitive state + abstract declaration

> "**I understood** — not in words, not in thought, but in the body, in the chest, in the hands — that the crusade was over and the thing that replaced it was not what we had fought for and **that this was the end**."

**Type:** Named cognitive state ("I understood") + abstract declaration ("this was the end").
**Location:** Line 99, packing scene.
**Problem:** The paragraph before this sentence already renders the moment perfectly: berets going into packs, caps staying on heads, morning light moving across the floor. The body ALREADY knows. "I understood... that this was the end" restates what the scene showed. "This was the end" is a thesis declaration.
**Fix:** Cut the entire sentence. The preceding rendered details (berets into packs, caps with yoke-and-arrows) carry the full weight. End the paragraph on: "The red wool disappearing into canvas, between blankets, under shirts. One by one. Man by man." Or if the refrain must land: "The body knew. The hands knew. The beret went into the pack."

## Violations — Omniscience / POV Leaks

### 14. Line 11 — Generalization beyond narrator's experience

> "the face of a professional soldier watching a war end **the way wars end**"

**Type:** Omniscience leak / generalization.
**Location:** Line 11, Vidal description.
**Problem:** Martín at 24 has fought in one war. "The way wars end" claims knowledge of a pattern across multiple wars. Even as a 56-year-old narrator, this generalization moves from specific perception (Vidal's face) into universal commentary that exceeds close-third POV.
**Fix:** Cut "the way wars end." → "the face of a professional soldier watching a war end — not with a final battle but through surrenders and retreats" (wait, that reintroduces the banned construction). Better: "the face of a professional soldier watching surrenders accumulate and radio announcements grow longer as the territory grew smaller."
