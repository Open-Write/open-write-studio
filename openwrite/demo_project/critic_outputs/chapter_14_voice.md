## 1. Voice Spec Compliance (V1-A: The Immediate Reckoning)

### POV & Tense
First-person retrospective with present-tense writing frame and past-tense remembered events. **Correct.** The chapter alternates between the grounding location ("I am writing this at the table. Inkwell half-full.") and the remembered war's end. This is exactly the locked spec's architecture.

### Immediacy Collapse Technique
The spec requires the narrator to re-enter memory through sensory bridges. Chapter 14 executes this precisely:

- **Line 125:** "The pen moves. The boy moves with it. I am twenty-four in the courtyard, beret in hand. I am fifty-six at the table, pen in hand. The same hands. Separated by thirty-two years and a war and a peace." — Textbook immediacy collapse. The hands bridge the gap. **Excellent.**
- **Line 129:** "I held the crucifix. I held it as I hold it now." — Past-present bridge through the same object. **Correct.**
- **Line 71:** "The body does things the mind has not sanctioned." — The body as bridge between remembered and remembering self. **Correct.**

The technique is varied (touch, weight, visual) and does not repeat the same bridge mechanism. No monotony risk.

### Grounding Location
The writing frame uses the hardware store table, inkwell, crack in the wood, alley window. **Correct.** Line 5: "my thumb finds it without looking, the way a tongue finds a broken tooth" — the grounding objects are rendered through physical habit, not description. The spec's key phrases are echoed without being copied.

### The Numbness Insight
The spec identifies the numbness as the novel's moral center. Chapter 14 renders it through behavior rather than statement:

- **Line 27:** "I did not cheer. I did not weep. I stood by the wall." — The numbness as posture.
- **Line 33:** "I understood for the first time that those were the same thing and that the understanding changed nothing." — The numbness as cognitive state.
- **Lines 91-98:** The beret-folding sequence. Men putting away the red wool without ceremony. The numbness as collective action.

The insight is not stated ("the numbness was...") but rendered through what Martín does and does not do. **Correct. The spec warns against editorializing; this chapter avoids it.**

### Writing-as-Reckoning Frame
The spec says the act of writing is itself moral labor. Line 125-126: "I am twenty-four in the courtyard, beret in hand. I am fifty-six at the table, pen in hand." The reckoning is the chapter's structure — the older man looking at the younger man's moment of ending. **Correct.**

## 2. Character Voice Registers

### Martín — The Fracture (Act V)

The character profile specifies Register 4 (The Fracture) for the final act: "Fragments. Questions without answers. The syntax of a man whose framework has collapsed."

- **Line 79:** "I held the crucifix. I did not pray. I did not speak." — Fragments. The catechism is gone. **Correct.**
- **Line 99:** "not in words, not in thought, but in the body, in the chest, in the hands" — The body replacing the framework. **Correct register, but see Tier 1 violation below.**
- **Line 127:** "The beret went into the pack. That is what happened. That is what I saw." — Declarative fragments. The man who can only report what the body did. **Correct.**

The Fracture register surfaces in private moments only (profile: "never in front of the men"). The chapter respects this — Martín's fragmentation is interior; his public face is silence ("I nodded. He went back inside."). **Correct.**

### Salazar — The Private Man (Act V)

The profile specifies Register 3 (The Private Man) for rare moments of honesty: "Quiet, reflective, almost conversational." This register appears only twice in the novel — once in Act III, once in Act V.

- **Line 51:** "We built a new Spain." — Not the Falangist register (no ideology, no José Antonio vocabulary). Not the Pragmatist (no enforcement). The Private Man. **Correct.**
- **Line 59:** "José Antonio said we would build a new Spain. He did not say the new Spain would look like this." — This is the exact line from the character profile's arc description. The Private Man register in its second and final appearance. **Correct.**

The narrative observes: "Not the Falangist register — the sharp, rhythmic cadence of ideology. Not the Pragmatist — the clipped sentences of a man enforcing an order. This was the Private Man." (Line 53.) This is Martín identifying the register shift, which is permitted within first-person POV — Martín has observed Salazar long enough to distinguish his voices.

**One concern:** The explicit register-name reference ("This was the Private Man") risks meta-address. The format rules ban "the narrator stepping back to explain the novel's own structural choices." However, this reads as Martín's private categorization of a man he has known for three years, not as the narrator explaining craft. **Borderline. Recommend evaluation.** If flagged as meta-address, the line can be rewritten to render the observation through Martín's perception without naming the register.

### Dialogue Tags

All dialogue uses "said" as default. No adverbial tags. No attribution excess. **Correct per format rules VIII.**

## 3. Format-Rule Compliance

### Tier 1 Violations (Scrub on Sight)

**VIOLATION 1 — "Not X. Not Y. But Z." construction (Line 99):**
> "not in words, not in thought, but in the body, in the chest, in the hands"

This is the banned "Not X. Not Y. Z." construction. The format rules state: "Banned in any form."

**Fix:** Rewrite to lead with what IS rather than what is NOT. Example: "I understood it in the body, in the chest, in the hands — the way the body understands what the mind cannot name."

**VIOLATION 2 — "Not X. Not Y. But Z." construction (Line 123):**
> "not destroyed, not disproven, just gone, the way a word goes when no one speaks it anymore"

Same banned construction.

**Fix:** "The words were gone — not destroyed, not disproven, just gone" becomes "The words had vanished the way a word vanishes when no one speaks it anymore." Lead with the state, not the negation.

**VIOLATION 3 — "The particular X of Y" construction (Line 83):**
> "the particular exhaustion of men who have been waiting for something and now have it and do not know what to do"

"The particular X of Y" is banned per Tier 1.

**Fix:** "the exhaustion of men who have been waiting..." — Remove "particular." The specificity of the relative clause already earns the description.

### Tier 2 Findings

**Em-dash overuse:** The chapter contains at least 8 em-dash-elaboration constructions across 135 lines (~4 pages). That exceeds the 2-per-page threshold. Instances include:

- Line 5: "the way a tongue finds a broken tooth"
- Line 47: "the hybrid uniform of the unified party"
- Line 53: "the sharp, rhythmic cadence of ideology"
- Line 61: "The face of a man who has delivered a parcel to the wrong address"
- Line 91: "He held it for a moment"
- Line 107: multiple em-dashes in the "Red like" passage

**Recommendation:** Reduce to ≤2 per page. Several can be converted to separate sentences or removed entirely. The em-dashes in the Salazar passage (lines 47, 53) are doing characterization work and can stay; the decorative ones (lines 5, 91) should be cut or restructured.

### Tier 1 — Triadic Parallel (Borderline)

**Line 107:** "Red like the Carlist flag. Red like the blood on the ridge. Red like the sunrise over the Ebro..." — This is a 5-element anaphora, not a triadic parallel. The format rules ban "Triadic parallel as default rhythm" and permit three beats "when the moment specifically calls for them." This passage has five beats and is the chapter's emotional climax. **Not a violation, but monitor.** The repetition is earned by the moment — the beret's color carrying the weight of the entire war. If the em-dash count is reduced elsewhere in the chapter, this passage's rhythmic intensity will stand out more effectively.

### Tier 1 — "There was" / "There is"

Not found. **Clean.**

### Tier 1 — Named Emotions

Not found. The chapter renders all interiority through physical detail and behavior. "Grief and celebration were the same shape that night" (line 39) is rendered consciousness, not named emotion. **Clean.**

### Literary Clichés (Banned List)

None of the banned phrases found. **Clean.**

### Prose Distance

The chapter modulates distance well:
- **Extreme close-up:** "my thumb finds it without looking, the way a tongue finds a broken tooth" (line 5). "I could feel the stitches under my thumb" (line 109).
- **Middle distance:** The courtyard scenes, the Salazar conversation, the barracks packing.
- **Compressed lyric:** "Barcelona had fallen. The Republic was a corridor, then a strip, then a rumor." (line 7). "The war ended. We had won." (line 131).

Five consecutive paragraphs at the same distance: **Not found.** The prose varies throughout. **Clean.**

### Scene vs. Summary

Predominantly scene. The radio announcement, the bonfire, the Salazar exchange, the beret-folding — all rendered in scene. Summary passages (lines 7-9, lines 83-84) earn their place by compressing what scene would belabor. **Clean.**
