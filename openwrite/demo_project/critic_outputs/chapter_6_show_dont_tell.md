## Violation Log

### V1 — Line 15
> "He said it the way a man says the name of a town he has been assigned to — without enthusiasm, without resistance, with the acceptance of a body that goes where it is told."

**Type:** Telling-not-rendering / Authorial intrusion
**Problem:** The narrator explains what Tomás's delivery means instead of letting the flat delivery stand. "With the acceptance of a body that goes where it is told" is the narrator's interpretive gloss on a moment that is already rendered by the dialogue and the preceding sentence.
**Fix:** Cut everything after the em-dash. "He said it the way a man says the name of a town he has been assigned to." The flatness is already there.

### V2 — Lines 53–55
> "Sound defined Jarama."
> "Artillery was loudest — a distant press against the chest, a rumble that shook the sandbags and made the tin rattle. That sound was background. The body absorbed it the way a heartbeat is absorbed — present, constant, beneath notice."

**Type:** Authorial intrusion / Telling-not-rendering
**Problem:** "Sound defined Jarama" is a thesis statement — the narrator stepping back to announce the chapter's theme. The simile "the way a heartbeat is absorbed" explains the body's relationship to artillery noise instead of rendering it. The prose that follows (lines 57–59) already renders this relationship concretely: "Crack meant the bullet had passed. Whistle meant it was still coming."
**Fix:** Cut "Sound defined Jarama." Begin directly: "Artillery was loudest — a distant press against the chest." Cut the heartbeat simile. Let the concrete sound catalog that follows do the work.

### V3 — Lines 105–106

**Type:** Telling-not-rendering / Authorial intrusion

The qualifier "not with the conviction of the catechism" is a generalization about the nature of his belief.

### V5 — Lines 145–146

**Type:** Telling-not-rendering / Summary
**Problem:** This is compressed summary of a general practice, not a specific scene. It works as contrast to the trench confessions, but the final sentence — "the weight of the sin transferred to the formula of the words" — is an abstract conceptual claim about how confession works, not a rendered detail.
**Fix:** Cut the final sentence. The inventory of trivial sins (Mass, mother, impure thought) already contrasts with the trench confessions. Let the contrast be felt, not explained.

### V6 — Lines 172–173
> "God is with you," Padre Joaquín said. His voice was warm, measured, certain. But the certainty was slower. Words came as if from further away. "The cause is just. The Church has said so."

**Type:** Omniscience leak / Telling
**Problem:** Martín can hear the voice. He can note warmth, measure, slowness. But "the certainty was slower" is an interpretation of Padre Joaquín's internal state — certainty is a cognitive condition, not an audible quality. "Words came as if from further away" is borderline (it could be a spatial metaphor for the voice itself), but combined with "the certainty was slower," it reads as the narrator accessing Joaquín's interiority.
**Fix:** Cut "But the certainty was slower." Keep "Words came as if from further away" — it's a physical metaphor for the voice's quality. Let the dialogue's content and Martín's perception do the rest.

## Assessment

- Lines 65–193 are the chapter's core and are largely clean. The hearing of foreign voices (V2 area), the confession scene, the sniper sequence, and the burial are rendered with physical specificity and earned interiority.

**Weaknesses:**
- The narrator has a tendency to interpret after rendering. The pattern is: concrete detail → narrator explains what the detail means. This is the dominant failure mode (V4, V7, V9, V10, V11, V13).
- The final 15% (lines 236–277) is the weakest section. Four violations in 41 lines, all of the same type: the narrator stepping in to label, interpret, or explain what the rendered details already convey.
- The frame sections (lines 1–5, 273–277) are thin but brief enough not to constitute structural problems.

**Recommendation:** Address V10, V11, V12, V13 as priority — these are in the chapter's closing movement, where the reader's final impression is formed. The remaining violations are lower severity and can be addressed in a revision pass.
