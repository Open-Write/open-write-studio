## Verdict: FAIL

Multiple patterns exceed thresholds. The chapter has a clear structural intent — the repetition of "the same" mirrors Martín's homecoming perception, and the polysyndeton chains render cumulative grief — but the execution overshoots craft into mechanical repetition. The patterns are so dominant they flatten the prose rather than punctuating it.

---

## Findings

### Critical: "the same" — 45 instances (limit: 12)

This is the chapter's central device. It works in concept: Martín returns and measures everything against memory. But 45 instances in 4,377 words means one every ~97 words. The repetition loses its power by the halfway point and becomes wallpaper. Every noun gets the treatment: the same bell, the same clock, the same columns, the same crucifix, the same glasses, the same hands, the same chair, the same silence, the same chair, the same crucifix, the same room, the same ceiling.

**Recommendation:** Cut to ≤15. Reserve "the same" for the moments where the sameness *matters most* — the crucifix, the beret hook, the pen on the counter. Let other objects carry their repetition through implication or physical detail alone.

### Critical: Negative density — 25.8 per 1k words (limit: 15/1k)

The chapter loops through negative constructions: "did not ask," "did not tell," "did not pray," "did not move," "did not open," "did not change," "did not adjust." The pattern "He did not X" appears as a default rhythm. At this density, the prose becomes defined by absence rather than presence, which is thematically appropriate but mechanically excessive.

**Recommendation:** Cut negative constructions to ≤15/1k. Convert some to positive assertions. "He did not ask about the war" → "He asked about the store." "I did not pray" → stay (this one earns its negation). "He did not adjust the beret" → stay (structural payoff). Cut the redundant ones.

### Critical: Em-dashes — 51 (limit: ~28, or 2/page)

Nearly every descriptive passage uses em-dash elaboration. The pattern is: noun — em-dash — list of three attributes. "The sign — ECHEVERRÍA FERRETERÍA — was the same sign." "His steps were slower than I remembered — the same economy, the same directness, but slower." "The staircase was narrow — the same worn treads, the same handrail." This is the AI elaboration pattern flagged in Tier 2.

**Recommendation:** Cut to ≤25. Replace half with commas, colons, or sentence breaks. Reserve em-dashes for true interruptions and sharp pivots.

### Advisory: Polysyndeton chains — 14 instances

The "and...and...and" chain is used as the chapter's secondary rhythm, especially in the final third. Some are earned (the final paragraph's accumulation of distances). Others are mechanical: "We walked through the store and through the back room and through the kitchen and into the dining room."

**Recommendation:** Cut to ≤7. Break the weaker chains with commas or semicolons.

### Advisory: "The" starters — 43% of sentences

Nearly half the chapter's sentences begin with "The." This creates a cataloguing rhythm — inventory prose — that works in small doses but dominates the chapter. The effect is flattening: every sentence has the same syntactic shape.

**Recommendation:** Vary sentence openings. Convert some "The X was Y" to constructions that lead with verbs, pronouns, or prepositions. Target ≤30%.

### Advisory: Structural triplets — 19 instances

The three-beat list is the chapter's default unit: "Not a question. Not an exclamation. A naming." / "I got up. I put on my boots. I went down the stairs." / "Not fight. Not win. Finish." Many are effective. But at 19 instances, the triplet becomes predictable.

**Recommendation:** Cut to ≤10. Let some moments land as single sentences or pairs.

### Minor: Named emotion — "felt" (2 instances)

"I felt the muscles of my neck tighten" (L47) — borderline, the physical detail follows. "The taste went into me and behind it was every meal" (L85) — rendered consciousness, not named emotion. Acceptable.

## Summary

The chapter's concept is sound — a homecoming rendered through obsessive measurement of sameness and absence. But the execution relies too heavily on a small set of mechanical patterns (the same, em-dash elaboration, polysyndeton, "The" starters, negative constructions) that compound into monotony. The patterns need to be thinned so the remaining instances land with force. At 45 uses, "the same" is invisible. At 12, it would be a knife.

**Priority cuts:**
1. "the same": 45 → ≤15
2. Negative density: 25.8/1k → ≤15/1k
3. Em-dashes: 51 → ≤25
4. Polysyndeton: 14 → ≤7
5. "The" starters: 43% → ≤30%
6. Triplets: 19 → ≤10
