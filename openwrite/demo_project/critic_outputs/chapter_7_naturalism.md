## Verdict: CONDITIONAL PASS

The chapter's observational prose is strong — the narrator registers physical detail with precision, the landscape descriptions are earned, and the emotional restraint is consistent with the voice. However, the "The same X" anaphora and pervasive triplet constructions create a mechanical rhythm that undermines naturalism at scale. The pattern is thematically motivated (the narrator finding sameness between Navarre and occupied Basque Country), but at current density it reads as authorial habit rather than character perception.

## Detailed Findings

### 1. "The same X" Anaphora — DOMINANT TIC (27 occurrences)

This is the chapter's primary naturalism risk. 27 instances of "The same" across 4,668 words — roughly once per 173 words. Seven are full anaphoric triplet sequences (three or more parallel "The same X" sentences):

| Lines | Sequence |
|-------|----------|
| L29 | The same houses... The same as in Aoiz. The same construction, the same materials, the same style |
| L61 | The same face. The same crown. The same tilt of the head... |
| L69 | The same workshops. The same tradition. The same Christ. |
| L157 | The same light. The same air. The same birds. |
| L189 | The same Mass. The same words. The same body and blood. |
| L215 | The same Mass. The same words. The same body and blood. (verbatim repeat of L189) |
| L243 | The same wood. The same paint. The same tilted head. The same half-closed eyes. The same God. |

**Issues:**
- L189 and L215 are identical — verbatim repetition of the same triplet sequence.
- L243 extends to five items, past the point of emphasis into list-making.
- The pattern is so frequent it ceases to register as perception and becomes a compositional default.

**Recommendation:** Reduce to 10–12 total "The same" instances. Cut the L189/L215 duplication — choose one location. Vary the articulation: "His corpus and theirs were carved by the same hands" achieves the same recognition without the anaphoric frame.

### 2. Triplet Constructions — HIGH (14 distinct sequences)

Beyond "The same," seven additional anaphoric triplet patterns:

| Lines | Pattern | Example |
|-------|---------|---------|
| L71 | Prayers X. Prayers Y. Prayers Z. | Prayers in Euskara. Prayers to the same God. Prayers for rain... |
| L69 | I could see X. I could see Y. I could see Z. | I could see the grain... I could see where the polychrome had cracked... |
| L171 | He studied X. He studied Y. He studied Z. | He studied my face. He studied my beret. He studied the pin... |
| L211 | It is the language of X. It is the language of Y. | It is the language of this place. It is the language of the confessional... |
| L213 | He looked at X. He looked at Y. He looked at Z. | He looked at the church. He looked at the mountains... |
| L235 | I think of X. I think of Y. I think of Z. | I think of the old man... I think of his hands... I think of the woman... |
| L237 | Now I know X. Now I know Y. Now I know Z. | Now I know that the town was a mirror... |

Additionally, comma-separated triplets appear at L37 (rifles slung, berets on, packs heavy), L43, L59, L93, L97, L103, L125, L135, L171, L183, L221, L223, L229.

**Total triplet density:** One triplet sequence approximately every 333 words. This is too regular for naturalistic prose. The reader begins to anticipate the pattern, which breaks the illusion of unmediated perception.

**Recommendation:** Eliminate 4–5 of the 7 anaphoric sequences. Keep the strongest (L69 corpus recognition, L171 old man's study, L213 Padre Joaquín's look). Convert others to varied syntax: "He studied my face, then the beret, then the pin" achieves the same accumulation without the hammering rhythm.

### 4. Em-dash — BORDERLINE (15 occurrences)

15 em-dashes in 4,668 words = 1 per 311 words. Not yet at crisis level, but approaching the threshold where em-dashes become a default connective rather than an emphatic choice.

**Distribution:** Clustered in the first half (L5, L31×2, L61, L75, L77×2, L93, L97, L103, L105×2, L125, L149, L155). The second half of the chapter uses none.

**Most effective uses:** L75 ("The plaza opened before us — stone, a fountain, the old man still on the bench") — the em-dash earns its weight as a perceptual arrival. L97 ("Some were broken — legs snapped, tops splintered") — physical specificity after the dash.

**Weakest uses:** L31 ("it passed — three seconds, four — and then") — parenthetical em-dash pair feels self-conscious. L61 ("It hung above the dining table in my father's house — a smaller version") — could use a period.

**Recommendation:** Cut 3–4 em-dashes. The L31 parenthetical pair and L61 are the easiest removals.

### 5. Polysyndeton — MODERATE (14 lines with 3+ "and")

The worst offender is L13, a single sentence with 9 "and" conjunctions describing the truck ride. L5 has 4 "and"s in the green landscape description. L243 has 6 "and"s in the closing passage.

**Recommendation:** L13 should be broken into shorter sentences. The ride into the mountains is one of the chapter's strongest passages, but the sentence runs out of breath. L243's 6 "and"s in the closing work as incantation — keep.

### 6. "The" Sentence Starters — HIGH (87)

87 sentences begin with "The" after punctuation. Combined with the "The same" anaphora, this creates a subject-first monotony. Many could begin with verbs, prepositions, or character actions instead.

## What Works (Naturalism Strengths)

- **Physical specificity without interpretation.** The corpus description (L59), the Euskara alphabet chart (L103–105), the old man's hands (L167) — all rendered through concrete detail without the narrator naming his emotions.
- **The flag scene (L121–137).** The narrator's body does the emotional work: crouching, picking up, smoothing, the tight throat, the hand on the wall. No interiority beyond "I did not understand why."
- **Tomás's restraint.** His minimal dialogue ("This looks like home," "Beans," "You all right?") and his silence at the crucifix and at the school doorway are earned naturalism.
- **Padre Joaquín's confession (L219).** "I do not know how to hold both in one hand" — the chapter's thematic thesis delivered as character speech, not authorial statement.
- **The closing frame (L227–243).** The return to the hardware store, the chart in the drawer, the old man's eyes in the room — temporal layering handled cleanly.

## Priority Fixes

1. **Cut 12–15 "The same" instances.** Eliminate the L189/L215 duplication. Convert remaining triplets to varied syntax. Target: 10–12 total.
2. **Eliminate 4–5 anaphoric triplet sequences.** Keep L69, L171, L213. Convert the rest.
3. **Reduce negative density in L41–47.** Replace 2–3 negatives with affirmative description.
4. **Cut 3–4 em-dashes.** Target L31 pair and L61.
5. **Break L13** into two sentences.
6. **Vary sentence openings.** Convert 10–15 "The" starters to verb-first or prepositional openings.
