# Chapter 10 Plan — "The Cold" (ACT FOUR OPENS)

---

## 1. Chapter Number and Title
Chapter 10 — "The Cold"

## 2. Characters Present
- **Martín Javier Echeverría** (POV) — The Soldier, The Fracture
- **Tomás Eguía** — The Companion, The Brother
- **Padre Joaquín** — The Doubt (visible, aging)
- **Teniente Rodrigo Vidal** — The Officer, The Realist

## 3. POV Character
Martín Javier Echeverría. First-person retrospective with immediacy technique.

## 4. Narrative Distance
Extreme close-up. This chapter demands claustrophobia — the world reduced to a trench, a blanket, a rifle, and the cold. The body is the building.

## 5. Outline Summary
December 1937–February 1938. Teruel. The Republic's first major offensive catches the Nationalists by surprise. The requeté are sent to hold the line in the worst cold in living memory — twenty degrees below zero. Men freeze in trenches. Rifles jam. The ground is iron. Martín survives because Tomás keeps him alive — sharing blankets, digging deeper, refusing to let him sleep in the open. The fighting is house-to-house in a frozen town. The requeté take casualties. Padre Joaquín, aging and thin, moves through the lines blessing the frozen dead. His blessings are rote. His breath is visible. He does not sit down because he is afraid he will not get up.

## 6. Emotional Palette
**Desperation** and **Numbness.** The reader should feel the cold — and the erasure of everything that is not survival. No theology here. No crusade. Only survival.

## 7. Key Beats
1. The cold — twenty degrees below zero. The ground is iron. Rifles jam.
2. Tomás keeps Martín alive — sharing blankets, digging deeper, refusing to let him sleep.
3. House-to-house fighting in a frozen town.
4. Padre Joaquín blessing the frozen dead — rote, visible breath, not sitting down.
5. The world reduced to survival.

## 8. Word Count Target
4,000–5,000 words.

## 9. Body Anchors
Skin (the cold — PRIMARY), hands (frozen, shaking, the rifle), breath (visible, the cold entering the lungs).

## 10. Callbacks
- **Tomás as protector** — keeping Martín alive. (Payoff: Ch12 — he cannot keep himself alive)
- **Padre Joaquín's rote blessings** — the blessing is the same, the certainty is gone. (Payoff: Ch13)

---

## Notes for the Prose Writer

1. **The cold is the chapter.** Render it through skin, breath, hands, the body's response. The reader must feel it.
2. **Tomás keeps Martín alive.** The companion's role is protective. The reader must feel the weight of what will be lost.
3. **Padre Joaquín does not sit down.** He is afraid he will not get up. Render through behavior.
4. **No theology. No crusade. Only survival.** The chapter is deliberately claustrophobic.
5. **All meta-critic constraints apply.** Chapter endings, "I did not know" (max 1), "the way" (≤2/1k), polysyndeton (≤25%), "The" starters (≤15%), em-dashes (≤2/250w), "the same" (≤12), negative density (≤10/1k), no "Not X but Y."
