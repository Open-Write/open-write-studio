## Pattern Analysis

### P1. Retrospective Narrator Interpretive Commentary — PERSISTENT, WORSENING

The narrator's tendency to explain what rendered scenes already convey remains the manuscript's most damaging pattern. Every chapter's worst writing is in the final 15%.

| Chapter | Violations in Final 15% | Total SDT Findings | Key Problem |
|---------|------------------------|-------------------|-------------|
| Ch7 | 8 of 20 | 20 | Lines 231-243: "Now I know that the town was a mirror," "He will learn. The war will teach him." |
| Ch8 | 0 of 3 blocking | 14 (3 blocking) | Clean ending — the one exception. Tomás's dialogue and writing frame earn their place. |
| Ch9 | 2 critical + 3 advisory/minor | 20 (2 critical, 8 advisory, 10 minor) | Lines 161-163: omniscient historical exposition + POV shift to "the boy." Line 183: "the silence is the answer and the answer is not enough." |

**Trajectory:** Ch7 is worst. Ch8 is clean (the only chapter that trusts its ending). Ch9 regresses — the "the boy" third-person shift is a new violation type not seen before.

**New rule:** The final 200 words of every chapter must be reviewed by the show-don't-tell critic as a discrete passage. Any passage that names emotions, explains scenes, delivers political thesis, or shifts POV = auto-fail.

The refrain returned in Ch9 after being absent in Ch7. The Ch9 instances use a new form ("the boy did not know" in third person) that compounds the P1 violation. Three instances in two lines is a refrain loop, not a refrain.

### P3. Triplet/Triadic Parallel as Default Rhythm — WORSENING

| Chapter | Triplet Lines | Polysyndeton Lines | Total (overlap) | Status |
|---------|--------------|-------------------|-----------------|--------|
| Ch7 | 14 | 14 | ~20 | High |
| Ch8 | ~20-25 | 2 | ~22 | Advisory |
| Ch9 | 41 | 32 | ~55 | **FAIL** |

**Trajectory:** Steadily worsening. Ch9 uses triplets and polysyndeton as its primary and secondary rhythmic devices. 41 triplet lines + 32 polysyndeton lines in 183 lines of prose means most lines participate in at least one pattern. The chapter reads as having one rhythm.

The moments that should land hardest (the stump, the letter, the final silence) are diluted because they use the same devices as the boot descriptions and terrain summaries.

**Rule:** Triplet closings: maximum 10 per chapter in non-scenic passages. Polysyndeton chains: maximum 8 lines with 3+ "and." Reserve incantatory rhythm for 3-4 key passages that earn it.

### P6. Omniscience Leaks — PERSISTENT (each chapter)

| Chapter | Leaks | Location | Type |
|---------|-------|----------|------|
| Ch7 | 5 (SDT) / 0 (Continuity) | Lines 171, 185, 191, 231, 235 | Interiority attribution to old man, woman, Padre Joaquín |
| Ch8 | 1 blocking | Line 145 | Padre Joaquín's private assessment ("never yet found it adequate") |
| Ch9 | 1 critical | Line 161 | Future historical exposition (Decree of Unification consequences) |

**Disagreement pattern:** Ch7's show-don't-tell found 5 omniscience leaks; continuity found 0. The disagreement is about interpretive language — show-don't-tell treats "His eyes said what his mouth did not" as a mind-read; continuity treats it as Martín's inference. The show-don't-tell reading is more conservative and should be authoritative when disagreement occurs.

**Rule:** "He saw X," "the certainty was slower," "the pragmatism of a man who..." = all violations. Interpreting observable behavior is permitted; attributing decisions, knowledge, or private assessments is not.

## Cross-Chapter Arc Assessment

### What's Working

- **Palette delivery:** Every chapter delivers its target emotions. Recognition → Moral vertigo → Exhaustion is the correct Act III descent.
- **Voice consistency:** The locked voice holds. Character registers differentiate cleanly. Amaia (Ch8) is the novel's strongest secondary character.
- **"Not X but Y" resolved:** The banned construction is absent across all three chapters.
- **Callback advancement:** Padre Joaquín's blessing trajectory (Ch7 dual blessing → Ch8 absolution → Ch9 stump blessing) is the strongest callback arc in the manuscript.
- **Physical specificity:** All critics praise the concrete detail — the boots, the chart, the stump, the letter's handwriting degradation.

### What's Failing

- **Rhythmic monotony (Ch9):** The combination of triplets + polysyndeton + negatives creates a single register that flattens emotional range. The chapter that needs the most variety has the least.
- **Narrator trust (Ch7, Ch9):** The narrator does not trust the reader to understand what rendered scenes mean. Two of three chapters collapse into thesis/explanation in the final 15%.
- **Continuity (Ch8):** Hard factual errors (chart, building, desks) that break reader trust. Must be fixed before advancing.
- **State file maintenance:** Systemic failure. The tracking files that enable chapter planning are 4 chapters behind.
