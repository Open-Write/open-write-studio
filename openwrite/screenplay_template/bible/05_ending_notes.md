- **The melancholy reading.** [What a melancholy audience takes away. Example: "The pause is over. The piano is what humanity made before it ended."]

- **The fourth reading.** [A quieter, more personal reading. Example: "Whatever is on the other side of dying is not lesser than what the beings became. The loss was a passage, not a wound."]
