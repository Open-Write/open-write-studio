### [CHARACTER NAME] — [one-line description]

**What they are.** [Role in the story. Not a job title — a dramatic function. Example: "A localized expression of the faction-debate, sent to communicate. It is not one being. It is a translation interface."]

**What they look like.** [Physical description. How do other characters perceive them? Note if their appearance changes per perceiver. Example: "To Mira: a woman of nineteen who has Sofia's eyes. To Theo: similar, but he sees her differently."]

**Voice.** [How they speak. Sentence length, vocabulary, rhythm. Example: "Patient. Tired. Warm in a way that does not soothe. Asks more questions than it answers."]

**Voice registers.** [What emotional registers does this character have? How does their voice change under different emotional conditions? Characters should have 2-4 distinct registers that emerge under different conditions. The richest moments happen when one register is speaking but another is bleeding through. Example:
- **The Scientist** — precise, controlled, converts emotion into data. Surfaces under intellectual pressure.
- **The Mother** — raw, grief-frozen, leaks through when the Scientist is under pressure. Surfaces in private moments.]

**Internal conflict — the visible layer.** [What does the audience see? Example: "Sometimes its language slips between 'we should help' and 'we should not.' These are not mistakes. They are the council leaking through."]

**What they hide.** [What is the character hiding, even from themselves? Example: "Beneath the visible split, the character has a quieter register. It watches mortal life with something close to envy."]
