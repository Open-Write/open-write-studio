# Editorial Review Protocol

*Pre-script review by 3 editorial personas. Catches structural problems when they're cheap to fix (in the outline) rather than expensive (in 120 pages of script).*

---

## Purpose

Review the outline/bible BEFORE any screenplay generation. This protocol is the first step in any production pipeline that involves structural complexity — no generation begins until the outline passes editorial review.

**Key principle:** Every hour spent in editorial review saves ten hours of script revision. Structural problems in an outline cost a paragraph to fix. The same problem in a 120-page script costs a week.

---

## The Three Personas

### Lara Marsh — Contest/Studio Reader (14 years)

- **Focus:** Cold coverage of the outline as a structural document
- **Questions:** Does the story work? Are the acts balanced? Is the structure viable? Where does the reader lose the thread? What's missing?
- **Output:** Coverage document with verdict (Recommend / Consider / Pass) and specific structural issues
- **Calibration:** Tired-but-fair. Calibrated against literary character drama. Describes what the script IS ABOUT, not what it was MEANT to be about.

### Dr. Elena Vasquez — Literary Fiction Editor (20 years)

- **Focus:** Thematic architecture
- **Questions:** Are the thematic tracks in conversation? Does the structure serve the themes or fight them? Is the thematic argument coherent? Does the work earn its ambition?
- **Output:** Editorial assessment with verdict and specific thematic/structural issues
- **Calibration:** Literary fiction sensibility. Evaluates whether the work earns its ambition. Looks for thematic shortcuts and unearned moments.

### Marcus Webb — Development Executive (12 years)

- **Focus:** Marketability and producibility
- **Questions:** Can this be shot? What's the budget implication? Who's the audience? Is the structure commercially viable or alienating? Where are the act breaks?
- **Output:** Development notes with verdict and specific producibility/marketability concerns
- **Calibration:** Commercial sensibility without being reductive. Evaluates whether ambition serves the audience or alienates them.

---

## The Protocol

1. **Present** each outline option to each persona independently (no persona sees another's feedback)
2. **Each persona produces coverage** — verdict + specific issues
3. **Synthesize feedback** across all three personas — identify overlapping concerns and unique perspectives
4. **Revise the outline** addressing the synthesized feedback
5. **Re-present** the revised outline to all three personas
6. **Iterate** until all three personas return positive verdicts
7. **Lock the outline** — no further structural changes after this point

---

## Structural Gate Requirements

Before the outline can be locked, it must pass ALL of these checks:

- **Act structure** — every act has a distinct function, escalation is earned, no act is a placeholder
- **Causal logic** — every plot event follows from prior causes, no authorial convenience
- **Arc completion** — every principal character's arc resolves, arcs are not dropped
- **Callback setup/payoff** — every callback seed has a payoff target, every payoff has a prior seed
- **Character architecture** — every principal character has: motivation, contradiction, blind spot, interiority method, and at least 2 voice registers defined
- **Knowledge consistency** — no character acts on information they don't have access to
- **Producibility** — the script can be produced within a reasonable budget (no unfilmable scenes)

If any check fails, the outline is NOT ready for generation. Revise and re-present to the panel.

## Success Criteria

- All 3 personas return positive assessments (Recommend or equivalent)
- No structural issues of comparable severity remain unresolved
- All structural gate checks pass (see above)
- The locked outline becomes the generation blueprint

## Integration with the Production Pipeline

```
Outline Creation
  → Editorial Review (this protocol)
    → Outline locked
      → Voice testing (voice_experiment_protocol.md)
        → Voice locked
          → Scene-by-scene generation (existing 9-mode critic pipeline)
```

The existing adversarial reader (Lara Marsh) continues to operate on generated pages. The pre-script Lara reads the outline; the post-script Lara reads the pages. Different documents, same persona, consistent standards.

---

## Key Differences from Post-Generation Review

| Aspect | Pre-Script (this protocol) | Post-Generation (adversarial reader) |
|--------|---------------------------|--------------------------------------|
| Input | Outline/bible document | Full script pages |
| Cost of fixes | Paragraph-level | Scene/chapter-level |
| Personas | 3 specialized perspectives | 1 (Lara Marsh) |
| Focus | Structure, theme, marketability | Execution, voice, pacing |
| Timing | Before any generation | After draft completion |
| Iteration speed | Hours per round | Days per round |
