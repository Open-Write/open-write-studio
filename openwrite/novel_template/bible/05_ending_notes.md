### Design Principles

- All readings should be defensible from the text.
- None should be dismissable.
- The novel must not foreclose any reading.
- The final element (image, line, sound) carries the ambiguity. Trust it.
- Do not explain the ending in the text. The reader's interpretation is the reader's work.
