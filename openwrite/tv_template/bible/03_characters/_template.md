## III. VOICE REGISTERS

*Characters have multiple emotional registers — distinct ways of speaking and behaving that emerge under different conditions. The richest scenes happen when one register is speaking and another is bleeding through.*

### Register Map

| Register Name | What It Is | When It Surfaces | How It Sounds |
|-----------|-----------|-----------------|---------------|
| [Register 1 name] | [What this register is — e.g., "The competent professional"] | [Under what conditions?] | [How does the character sound when in this register?] |
| [Register 2 name] | [What this register is] | [Under what conditions?] | [How does the character sound?] |
| [Register 3 name] | [What this register is] | [Under what conditions?] | [How does the character sound?] |

> *Example for Lena:*
>
> | Register Name | What It Is | When It Surfaces | How It Sounds |
> |-----------|-----------|-----------------|---------------|
> | **The Analyst** | The competent professional. Cold, precise, systematic. | When she's working a case, briefing the team, in control. | Complete sentences. No contractions. Clinical vocabulary. "The subject exhibits pattern-consistent behavior." |
> | **The Frightened Child** | The terrified person who saw something she couldn't unsee. | When the pattern matches her childhood experience. Late at night. When she's alone. | Fragments. Questions. "I don't — that's not — it was the same." |
> | **The Deflector** | Keeps her functional. Suppresses the fear. Maintains the facade. | When someone gets too close. When the fear starts to surface. | Deflection. Humor. Subject changes. "That's not relevant. What about the victim's timeline?" |
> | **The Desperate** | Acts when the deflector fails. Reckless, impulsive, desperate. | When she feels trapped. When the pattern is too close. | Commands. Interruptions. "Stop. Just — stop. I need to go." |

### Centered State

> [What is this character like when their defenses are quiet? This is rare — most characters never fully let their guard down during the series. But it's what the audience is rooting for.]
>
> *Example: When Lena's defenses are quiet, she is curious, gentle, and funny. She likes bad puns and thunderstorms. She hasn't been this person in years.*

### Voice Rules for Writing This Character

> [Specific rules for how to write this character's dialogue and action.]
>
> *Example:*
> 1. *Never name the registers in action lines. "The Analyst takes over" is forbidden. "Her spine straightens. Her voice flattens." is allowed.*
> 2. *The frightened register never appears in public. It only surfaces when Lena is alone or with someone she trusts completely.*
> 3. *The deflector and desperate registers often clash in the same scene — the character starts with a deflection and ends with an outburst.*
> 4. *The analyst is her default mode. If she's on screen and not in crisis, the analyst is speaking.*
