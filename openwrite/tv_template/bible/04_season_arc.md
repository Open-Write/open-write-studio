## II. SEASON ARC STRUCTURE

> [Describe the overall arc of the season. What is the beginning, middle, and end? What are the major turning points?]
>
> *Use the act structure that fits your show. For a 10-episode season, a common structure is:*
>
> - **Episodes 1-3:** Setup. Introduce the world, the characters, the engine. Establish the status quo, then disrupt it.
> - **Episodes 4-6:** Complication. The stakes escalate. The protagonist's personal life collides with the case. Mid-season twist.
> - **Episodes 7-9:** Crisis. Everything the protagonist thought they knew is challenged. The cost of the investigation becomes personal.
> - **Episode 10:** Resolution (or refusal to resolve). The season question is answered — or the audience realizes the question was wrong.

### Act One — Episodes 1-3: [Title]

> [What happens in the first three episodes? What is established? What is the hook?]
>
> *Example:*
>
> *Episodes 1-3 establish Lena, the ABU, and the anomaly-of-the-week engine. Each episode is a standalone case with an anomalous element. In the background, Lena begins to notice that the anomalies share a statistical signature — one she recognizes from her retracted paper. By the end of Episode 3, she has confirmed: the pattern is real, it is accelerating, and it is heading toward something.*

### Act Two — Episodes 4-6: [Title]

> [What escalates? What is the mid-season twist?]
>
> *Example:*
>
> *Episodes 4-6 raise the stakes. The anomalies become more violent. Lena's personal life — her estranged sister, her insomnia, her medication — begins to fracture under the pressure. The mid-season twist in Episode 6: Lena discovers that the pattern's origin point is a real place — and she's been there before, as a child, the night her father died.*

### Act Three — Episodes 7-9: [Title]

> [What is the crisis? What does the protagonist stand to lose?]
>
> *Example:*
>
> *Episodes 7-9 are the crisis. Lena follows the pattern to its source and discovers something she cannot explain within the framework of her science. The institutional response — the Bureau wants to shut the unit down, the Pentagon is interested for the wrong reasons — forces Lena to choose between her career and the truth. Episode 9 ends with Lena alone, having made a choice that cannot be undone.*

### Act Four — Episode 10: [Title]

> [What is the resolution? Does the season question get answered?]
>
> *Example:*
>
> *Episode 10 resolves the season's case — the anomaly's source is identified and confronted — but refuses to resolve the season's question. Lena has the knowledge. What she does with it is Season 2's problem. The final image: Lena sitting in her car outside her sister's house, engine running, the pattern visible in the streetlights if you know how to look.*

## VIII. SEASON BOUNDARIES

> [What does this season NOT do? What is left for future seasons?]
>
> *Example:*
>
> 1. *Season 1 does not reveal what the pattern IS. It reveals that the pattern is real.*
> 2. *Season 1 does not heal Lena. It integrates her fear into her work.*
> 3. *Season 1 does not resolve the Bureau's institutional response. It sets up the conflict for Season 2.*
> 4. *Season 1 does not reveal what happened to Lena's father. It reveals that what she remembers is incomplete.*
